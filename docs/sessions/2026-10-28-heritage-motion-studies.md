# 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-10: Heritage motion studies (blackletter and weaving)

One long session, run across several days.

## What Noah asked for
A series of motion studies inspired by fafa_motion's calligraphy work, with old English blackletter and weaving in place of Chinese characters, rooted in his English heritage. Webster is an occupational surname meaning weaver (his confirmation).

## What happened, in order
1. Research: textura means "woven"; minims, quadrata feet and hairline joins make a page of textura read like cloth. Offered a loom-to-letter framing (cloth at 0, word at 1).
2. Built a rough Cavalry JS Shape plugin, Loom to Letter (`cavalry/loom-to-letter/`), and installed it. Noah: do it "more traditionally" than a plugin, as a mix of native Cavalry scenes, real type, hand-made sources and keyframes. The plugin is now only a sketch tool.
3. Format: chapter posts on Instagram, then a cut-down film. Endings are seamless loops. He wants a story, not just visuals.
4. A literal chronological story was rejected ("we dont have to be so literal"). Standing rule: no people or animals in any frame.
5. His format idea: a two-frame setup, real close-up footage of hand-work beside the Cavalry study that used that footage (Cavalry's Image Sampler reads video). Layouts vary per study: side by side, stacked, overlay, or an anime-style corner inset.
6. Footage search on Destockd (public domain clips). Noah picked and downloaded 13 clips himself into `~/Downloads/SIDE QRAFT`, along with about 80 fafa_motion references.
7. Fafa's assembling-units move is "part of it"; he wants more than one idea. Looked at the Oddfellows Figma anthem as a reference.
8. Scope: typography first, textiles and footage later. Fonts: Jacquard 24 (plus Charted, installed) and Canterbury (already installed; its licence allows commercial use but not modifying the font).
9. Rough board of five type studies and a font specimen in `cavalry/loom-to-letter/boards/`. Noah is mapping the studies in Figma himself.
10. Colour research, then a palette sheet sampled from real scans: Luttrell Psalter page, naturally dyed wool and the Unicorn Tapestries (`boards/heritage-palette.png` and `.json` with each colour's source).
11. Noah chose the page palette: ink #543E2F, vellum #D3CCB8, red #90624F, blue #304A82 (`boards/page-palette.png`). Red is 3.2 to 1 on vellum, so it suits capitals rather than text.
12. 2026-11-10: wrote a prompt asking for a spec brief for a fictional type archive ("The Royal English Type Archive", an invented name), saved in `cavalry/loom-to-letter/BRIEF-PROMPT.md`.
