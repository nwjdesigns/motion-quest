# 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-24: Type archive spec, the 17th century, and sourcing

One long session, run across several days. Continues the heritage motion studies lane (`2026-10-28-heritage-motion-studies.md`). Work files are in `cavalry/loom-to-letter/`, still untracked in this public repo on purpose.

## What happened, in order
1. The fictional "Royal English Type Archive" brief moved to a real body, as a self-initiated concept. Shortlist of ten real type archives built as a private slides deck (artifact `24LnSLjnuKhvZatgVE4qxt`), each with its current identity and colours sampled. First pick: the National Typefounding Collection (no identity at all).
2. Era board, `boards/type-eras.png` and `.json`: 27 Commons specimens from the 15th to the 20th century. Noah chose the 17th century: "modern enough but still old".
3. Standing direction, confirmed by Noah: **source is old, handling is new.** Real letters, scans and facts, never faked age (no distressing, paper overlays or foxing). The modern part is the grid, crop, scale, timing and motion.
4. The client was re-picked for the era: **the Fell types**, Oxford University Press Museum (roman, italic and blackletter in one real collection). `BRIEF.md` and the deck rewritten for it.
5. Blackletter history sourced and added to the brief. Two claims from earlier in the session were wrong and were corrected by sources: roman did not take over England early (blackletter stayed common until about 1700), and blackletter as "the letter of authority" is unsourced (the sourced reading is that it marked popular readers). Proclamations and statutes in blackletter are marked unconfirmed.
6. Sourcing, register at `cavalry/loom-to-letter/sources/SOURCES.md`:
   - On disk: the 1706 Fell specimen (51 pages), Reed's 1887 *Old English Letter Foundries*, Moxon's 1683 *Mechanick Exercises* (whole, 343 MB), and 50 chosen pages of the 1611 King James Bible.
   - View only: the 1686 and 1695 Fell specimens on Digital Bodleian. The photographs are Christ Church's and all rights are reserved. Noah said "Not yet" to writing to Christ Church.
   - Blocked: Folger (human check), the Oxford Text Archive (refused the connection).
7. Close-ups: full-size page scans (about 6,800 to 7,600 px) of the key Fell and Bible pages, and 15 crops, in `sources/closeups/`. They are microfilm scans, so they show ink on grey with film speckle, not paper colour.
8. 2026-11-24: the second half of the session became a separate design jam for an unbranded type archive, the National Typefounding Collection. It lives outside this repo, with its own notes.
